HOW TO PLAY

Dario "Río" Voss is back on Cayo Bravo. Don Aurelio Salas — the Harbormaster — is buying the whole island, pier by pier. Build your crew, run 8 story missions, and take the island back before the last ferry sails.

Drive: W accelerate · S brake/reverse · A/D steer · Space handbrake · E enter/exit any car.
On foot: WASD move · Shift run · F punch · Mouse aim, click to shoot · 1–5 switch weapon.
Crew: Z Swap Plates (−1 wanted) · X Quick Fix (repair car) · C Eyes Out (spot police).
Wanted: stars rise when police see crime. Break line of sight to cool down; at 4★ a helicopter joins the hunt.
Save: pause menu (P) or the garage — 3 slots, stored in your browser.
